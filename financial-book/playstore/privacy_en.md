# Privacy Policy

Goblin Note (the "app") is a budgeting app made by Wind Gripper (the "developer"). This policy explains what information the app keeps, where it keeps it, and what leaves your device.

Effective date: October 9, 2026

## 1. Summary

- The app has no sign-up, and the developer runs no server.
- What you record is stored encrypted on your device. The developer does not receive it and cannot read it.
- Only three features talk to servers over the internet: cloud backup, price and exchange rate lookup, and usage statistics. All three start off and run only after you turn them on.

## 2. What we never send

Whatever features you turn on, the data you record in the app and anything the app reads on your device are never sent to the developer or to the analytics service. That covers amounts and balances, the names of accounts, ledgers and budgets, transaction details, text messages and receipts, contacts, and anything about the family members or devices you share with. The app also never reads or sends the advertising ID.

If you turn on cloud backup, this data goes into an encrypted backup file in your own cloud storage (4-1). It does not go to the developer.

## 3. What stays on your device

- Your transactions, accounts, ledgers, budgets and settings are stored in an encrypted database on your device. The encryption key is kept in the device's secure key storage (Android Keystore).
- Fingerprint or face unlock is handled by the operating system. The app never receives biometric data.
- The app does not use Android Auto Backup, so your data is not copied to Google through device backup.
- Uninstalling the app or resetting it deletes the data on your device. The developer cannot recover it.

## 4. What leaves your device only when you turn it on

### 4-1. Cloud backup

- Where: an app-only folder in your own account with the cloud storage service you choose. The app cannot see or read any file outside that folder.
- What: encrypted backup files, and a key file locked with your recovery key. Without the recovery key, neither the developer nor the cloud storage service can open your backups.
- When you connect an account, the app stores only that account's display name and profile photo address on your device, so it can show which account is connected. It does not store your email address.
- The cloud storage service's own privacy policy applies to your use of it.
- To turn it off: switch the backup location back to this device, or remove the app's access in that service's account settings.

### 4-2. Price and exchange rate lookup

- Where: the price services you turn on. Korea Investment & Securities Open API (stocks), Finnhub (US stocks), Upbit (crypto), Frankfurter (exchange rates).
- What: the ticker and currency codes of what you hold. Quantities and amounts are never sent.
- For services that need a key, the key you obtained and entered is sent only to that service. It is stored encrypted on your device and never sent to the developer.
- As with any internet request, each service can see your device's IP address. Each service's own privacy policy applies.
- To turn it off: turn off price lookup in settings.

### 4-3. Usage statistics

- Usage statistics are optional. They start off, and nothing that happened before you turn them on is sent later.
- Where: Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC).
- What: which screens and features are used and how often, and whether features such as automatic entry or device pairing complete. Each record carries only a fixed category code, never a value you entered.
- What the analytics service also receives: a random number created for each installation, device model, operating system and app version, and an approximate location derived from your IP address.
- Not used for: the app does not read the advertising ID, and the data is never used for ads, ad personalization, or tracking you across other apps.
- Purpose: only to make the app easier to use.
- Retention: individual records are deleted after 2 months. Totals combined across many users (for example, the average number of entries per day) cannot be split back into one person's share, so they are kept.
- Automatically created identifiers: the app uses no cookies. The random number above is the only one, and it exists only while usage statistics are on. Turning them off deletes it.
- To turn it off: turn off "Send usage statistics" in settings. The app stops sending and deletes the data the analytics service kept on your device, including the random number.

## 5. Sharing between devices

- Paired devices exchange data directly over the same Wi-Fi network or a short-range wireless link. Nothing passes through a developer server, and the connection is encrypted.
- What goes to the other device is the ledger records you chose to share, plus member details such as your name and symbol. The other person can see what you send, so pair only with people you trust.
- With "Find on the same network" turned on, the app announces itself on your local network while it is open on screen. The announcement carries only a randomly generated name, never your records or your name.

## 6. Device permissions

Each permission is requested when you first use the feature that needs it. If you decline, everything else keeps working.

- Read and receive SMS: to enter transactions from bank and card text messages. Messages are processed on your device and never sent anywhere.
- Camera: to photograph receipts and scan pairing QR codes. Recognition happens on your device.
- Photos: to pick a receipt image.
- Nearby devices (location on Android 12 and earlier): to find nearby Wi-Fi devices. On Android 12 and earlier the operating system requires location permission for this. The app never reads or stores your location.
- Notifications: for budget alerts and similar notices.

## 7. Purchases

- Payments for paid features are handled by Google Play. Neither the developer nor the app receives your card number or other payment details.
- The app gets purchase records (product, purchase state, and a purchase token) from the Google Play app on your device to confirm what you own, and stores them on your device.
- The developer uses the order details Google Play provides (order number, product, country of purchase and the like) to handle refunds and questions, and to keep the records the law requires.

## 8. Feedback and questions

- "Send feedback" only opens your email app; the app does not send anything itself. The message is prefilled with the app version and Android version, which you can delete before sending.
- The developer uses your email address and message only to reply and to improve the app, and deletes them one year after the matter is closed.

## 9. Statistics from Google Play

The developer sees install counts, totals by country and device type, and crash and "app not responding" reports in the Google Play Console. Google collects crash reports only from devices whose owners chose to share diagnostics with Google. The developer receives this information in a form that does not identify individual users.

## 10. Service providers and international transfers

Only if you turn on usage statistics, the following data is transferred abroad:

- Recipient: Google LLC (privacy contact: https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- Countries: the United States and other countries where Google operates data centers
- When and how: over the internet while you use the app with usage statistics on
- Data: the "What" and "What the analytics service also receives" items in 4-3
- Purpose: usage statistics for the app
- Retention: individual records for 2 months
- How to refuse: leave usage statistics off, or turn them off. Every feature of the app works without them.

Cloud backup and price lookup are services you use directly with your cloud storage service and each price service. The developer does not hand your information to them.

## 11. Your rights

- You can view, change, export and delete the data on your device at any time.
- You can turn off usage statistics at any time. If you want your individual records deleted, contact us below. The developer holds nothing that identifies you, so we may be unable to find a specific record. Either way, individual records are deleted after 2 months.
- Agreeing to this policy is a condition of using the app. To withdraw, uninstall the app; the data on your device is deleted with it.
- You can contact or file a complaint with the data protection authority of the country you live in about how your personal information is handled.

## 12. Deletion

- Data on your device: deleted immediately when you uninstall or reset the app.
- Individual usage statistics records: deleted by the analytics service 2 months after collection.
- Feedback email: deleted one year after the matter is closed.
- Purchase records the law requires us to keep: kept for that period, then deleted.

## 13. How we protect your data

- The database on your device and your backup files are encrypted.
- Transfers between devices are encrypted and happen only with devices you paired.
- The app can be locked with fingerprint, face or your device lock.
- There is no server that gathers user data.

## 14. Children

The app is not directed at children under 14. If you are under 14, please do not turn on usage statistics. The app shows the same notice next to the usage statistics option.
